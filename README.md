# Hybrid-Physics-AI-SOC-Estimation-for-EV-Batteries-under-Indian-Climate-Conditions
# EV BMS SOC Estimation System (Simulink): the final project model

`EV_BMS_SOC_Estimation_System.slx` is the complete project in one model. Signal flow:

> EV drive cycle and Indian climate → vehicle dynamics → battery pack and BMS current limits → **nonlinear electro-thermal LG 18650HG2 battery** → BMS sensors (INA240, INA226, DS18B20) → **real-time SOC estimator at 1 Hz** (electro-thermal EKF + 1-D CNN + confidence-based adaptive fusion) → BMS protection and warnings

The model is generated by a script, so every block, port and signal is named.
Nothing in `D:\Matlab Drive` is changed.

## Run it (MATLAB, in this folder)

| Step | Script | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **`run_SOC_Estimation_Test`** | **The main demo, on measured data.** Runs the estimator on eight held-out LG HG2 recordings: the four test files (−10, 0, 10, 25 °C) and the extreme-temperature drives (40 °C UDDS, LA92, US06 and −20 °C UDDS). **Figure 1**: SOC of EKF, CNN and fusion against the reference. **Figure 2**: measured surface temperature, the EKF's core-temperature estimate, and shaded bands wherever the BMS raised a warning. Prints RMSE per method and the BMS warnings table, and writes `soc_test_results.csv` and `bms_warnings_test.csv`. |
| **2** | **`run_Extreme_Heat_Scenarios`** | **Rajasthan-type heat, 40–60 °C ambient (simulation).** Jaipur 40, Delhi 45, Rajasthan 50, Thar 55 and 60 °C drives; a car parked in the sun (pack at 58 °C); a sealed scooter pack climbing 6 % at 48 °C; fast charging at 45 and 50 °C. For each: SOC estimation, temperatures with the BMS warning / derating / cut-off bands, and the BMS current limits. |
| 3 | `run_Indian_Climate_Scenarios` | The full EV on the MIDC at −10 … 60 °C, a 6 % ghat climb, an e-scooter and fast charging. Shows temperatures, heat, Wh/km, BMS derating and the estimator's SOC error. |
| 4 | `run_Validation_LGHG2` | The battery model against measured data at −20 … 40 °C (voltage and surface temperature). |

Each script rebuilds the `.slx` automatically when it is missing or older than
the build script, `estimator_code.m` or the parameter files
(`ensure_model_built.m`). So after copying new files in, just run the script.

Requirements: MATLAB and Simulink only, with no extra toolboxes. The estimator
blocks load their constants (EKF tables, CNN weights, fusion settings) from
`SOC_Estimator_Params.mat` with `coder.load`. Keep that file in this folder.

## SOC estimation results (measured data, never used for training)

Each estimator runs causally at 1 Hz exactly as coded in the Simulink blocks.
The block code was checked against a line-by-line Python port: the EKF matches
the project's Python EKF exactly, and the CNN forward pass matches the trained
network to within 1e-16.

**The four test files (UDDS + LA92 + US06 discharges with recharges, 11–13 h each):**

| SOC RMSE (%) | −10 °C | 0 °C | 10 °C | 25 °C | **Mean** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coulomb counting | 4.79 | 4.45 | 15.79 | 4.71 | 7.44 |
| Previous isothermal EKF (for reference) | 4.05 | 2.74 | 2.91 | 2.35 | 3.01 |
| Electro-thermal EKF | 1.95 | 1.04 | 1.62 | 1.15 | 1.44 |
| 1-D CNN | 2.81 | 2.15 | 2.22 | 1.60 | 2.20 |
| Fixed fusion (α = 0.5) | 1.83 | 1.42 | 1.74 | 0.99 | 1.50 |
| **Adaptive fusion** | **1.90** | **1.05** | **1.60** | **1.09** | **1.41** |

**Extreme temperatures (separate recordings of the same cycles):**

| SOC RMSE (%) | 40 °C UDDS | 40 °C LA92 | 40 °C US06 | −20 °C UDDS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coulomb counting | 2.27 | 2.23 | 2.15 | 2.05 |
| Electro-thermal EKF | 1.17 | 1.23 | 2.17 | 6.06 |
| 1-D CNN | 3.28 | 3.28 | 4.26 | 2.60 |
| Fixed fusion (α = 0.5) | 2.10 | 2.09 | 2.90 | 4.00 |
| **Adaptive fusion** | **1.25** | **1.29** | **2.18** | **5.87** |

How to read these:

- **−10 … 25 °C:** the objective, **RMSE ≤ 2 % at every temperature, is met by adaptive fusion.**
- **40 °C:** the CNN was trained only at −10 … 25 °C, so here it is out of distribution. The fusion detects this and gives the EKF about 95 % of the weight, so the fused result follows the EKF (1.3 % on UDDS and LA92, 2.2 % on the aggressive US06).
- **−20 °C:** this is the weak point. The cell's behaviour there is strongly nonlinear, the EKF error is 6 %, and the fusion still trusts the EKF because the CNN is flagged as out of range. Say this openly. A CNN retrained with −20 and 40 °C data is the fix.
- **Coulomb counting** looks good on the short extreme-temperature recordings only because they start from a known full charge. It drifts on long tests (15.8 % at 10 °C).

The fusion weights (β = 1, ρ = 10) were calibrated on validation data only.
The CNN's 60 s current average is computed causally (trailing window) for real
time, which costs at most 0.06 % SOC.

## BMS warnings

The BMS (subsystem 6) uses the hotter of core and surface temperature:

| Condition | BMS action | Colour in the figures |
|---|---|---|
| T ≥ 45 °C | over-temperature **warning** | yellow |
| T ≥ 50 °C | **derating**: discharge limit falls linearly to zero at 60 °C; charging stopped | orange |
| T ≥ 60 °C | **cut-off**: no discharge, no charge | red |
| T < 0 °C while charging or regenerating | **charge blocked** (lithium-plating risk) | blue |
| 0–10 °C | charging at half rate | – |
| V ≥ 4.2 V, V ≤ 2.8 V, SOC ≤ 5 % | over-voltage, under-voltage, low-SOC flags | – |

On the measured data, the 40 °C recordings peak at 41 °C surface, so they do
not reach the 45 °C warning. The cold files show the blue band: regenerative
pulses during the drive happen below 0 °C (about 13 % of the time at −10 °C,
7 % at 0 °C, 22 % at −20 °C). The recharges in the test files were done at
25 °C, so they are allowed. The heat warnings appear in `run_Extreme_Heat_Scenarios`.

## Model structure (top level, left to right)

Subsystems 1–6 are the EV and battery plant. Subsystems 7–9 are the BMS sensors and the SOC estimator.

| Subsystem | Contents |
|---|---|
| `1_Drive_Cycle_and_Climate` | Vehicle speed (MIDC: 4 × ECE-15 urban + EUDC low-power at 90 km/h), road grade, ambient temperature profile, and a measured cell current input (validation mode) |
| `2_EV_Vehicle_Dynamics` | Aerodynamic, rolling (Indian-road C<sub>rr</sub>), grade and inertial forces → wheel power → battery power. Includes drivetrain efficiency, a regenerative-braking limit and HVAC/auxiliary load, which rises with heat for air-conditioning in Indian summers |
| `3_Pack_Current_and_BMS_Limits` | I<sub>pack</sub> = P / V<sub>pack</sub>(k−1), a mode switch (vehicle or measured current), the BMS dynamic current limiter, and the split into cell current (÷ N<sub>p</sub>) |
| `4_Cell_ElectroThermal_Model` | The nonlinear cell model (4a–4d below) |
| `4a_Parameter_Maps_SOC_Tcore` | 2-D tables over (SOC, T<sub>core</sub>) for OCV, R0, R1, τ1, R2, τ2. 1-D tables for the Butler–Volmer exchange current I0(T), hysteresis M(T), entropic dU/dT(SOC) and capacity factor(T) |
| `4b_Electrical_Nonlinear_Dynamics` | Nonlinear electrical states (SOC, V1, V2, h) and the heat sources (detail below) |
| `4c_Thermal_Model_Core_Surface` | Two-state thermal model with C<sub>c</sub>, C<sub>s</sub>, R<sub>c</sub>, R<sub>u</sub> (State-Space block). Outputs core and surface temperature |
| `4d_Ageing_SOH_Xu2018` | Xu et al. (2018) degradation stresses (depth of discharge, SOC, temperature, time). SOH feeds back into capacity and resistance |
| `5_Pack_Outputs_Energy` | V<sub>pack</sub> = N<sub>s</sub>·V<sub>cell</sub>, pack power, energy (kWh), distance (km) |
| `6_BMS_Protection_and_Derating` | Thermal warning, derating and cut-off, charge inhibit below 0 °C, half-rate charging at 0–10 °C, over/under-voltage and low-SOC flags (table above) |
| `7_BMS_Sensors_INA240_INA226_DS18B20` | Current gain error (0.5 %), offset (20 mA) and noise. Voltage noise and the 1.25 mV INA226 LSB. DS18B20 offset (+0.3 °C) and 0.0625 °C LSB. All sampled at 1 Hz |
| `8_Estimator_Input_Select` | Selects live sensors (EV scenarios) or recorded LG HG2 test data (`SCN.est_source = 2`). Converts the plant SOC (2.78 Ah C/20 basis) to the estimator's 3.15 Ah basis for scoring |
| `9_SOC_Estimator_EKF_CNN_Fusion_1Hz` | **9a** electro-thermal EKF, **9b** 1-D CNN, **9c** confidence-based adaptive fusion, described below |
| `Delay_*` (Memory blocks) | One-step delays that break the two algebraic loops (P/V and BMS limits) |
| `log_*` To Workspace and scopes | `log_vehicle`, `log_cell`, `log_thermal`, `log_heat`, `log_bms` (limits + 7 warning flags), `log_pack`, `log_soc` |

**Inside `4b_Electrical_Nonlinear_Dynamics`:**

- States: SOC, the two RC voltages V1 and V2, and the hysteresis state h.
- Terminal voltage:

      Vt = OCV + M·h − I·R0 − η_ct − V1 − V2

- Butler–Volmer charge-transfer overpotential:

      η_ct = (2RT/F) · asinh(I / 2I0)

- Heat sources, which are output separately:
  - ohmic: I²R0
  - charge transfer: I·η_ct
  - polarisation: I·(V1 + V2)
  - reversible: −I·T·dU/dT

**Inside `9_SOC_Estimator_EKF_CNN_Fusion_1Hz`:**

- **9a – Electro-thermal EKF.**
  - States: [SOC, V1, V2, T_core, T_surface, T_ambient].
  - Measurements: [V, T_surface].
  - Parameters come from (SOC × T_core) tables. The voltage model includes the same Butler–Volmer term as the plant. It falls back to the measured surface temperature after a thermal event the model cannot follow.
  - Outputs: SOC, estimated core temperature, the innovation, S, P_SOC and dOCV/dSOC.
- **9b – 1-D CNN.**
  - Input: a 240 s window of 7 channels: v, i, T, i_ma, ah_cum, dv and v + i·R0.
  - Architecture: Conv16k5–BN–ReLU–Pool, Conv32k5–BN–ReLU–Pool, Conv64k3–BN–ReLU, global average pooling, Dense32, Dense1 with sigmoid.
  - Size: 11,713 parameters.
  - It also outputs the out-of-distribution statistics (Mahalanobis d² and max |z|).
- **9c – Adaptive fusion.**
  - EKF confidence combines innovation consistency, covariance and observability.
  - CNN confidence combines stability, agreement with the Coulomb-counted ΔSOC, and being in-distribution.
  - α = ρ·c_EKF^β / (ρ·c_EKF^β + c_CNN^β), slew-limited.
  - Fused SOC = α·SOC_EKF + (1 − α)·SOC_CNN.
  - Fixed fusion and Coulomb counting are also output as baselines.

## Where the numbers come from

`ET_Battery_Params.m` is identified from the full McMaster LG 18650HG2 data at
−20, −10, 0, 10, 25 and 40 °C:

- **Identification:** the Mixed1–8 drive cycles at each temperature, plus the C/20 tests for capacity.
- **Held out:** UDDS, LA92, US06 and HWFET at every temperature. They are used only for validation and for the SOC tests above.

The estimator's EKF tables use the project's original tables at −10 … 25 °C and
blend into the full-data tables towards −20 °C and 40 °C.

**Within −20 … +40 °C** the parameters are fitted to measured data.

**From +40 to +80 °C and below −20 °C**, resistances and the exchange current
follow the fitted Arrhenius laws, and OCV, capacity and hysteresis are held at
the nearest measured temperature. This range is an extrapolation and should be
stated as such. No public data for this cell exist above 40 °C.

The thermal parameters are those of a single cell in a ventilated chamber. A
real pack cools more slowly; `apply_cooling.m` scales R<sub>u</sub> for that
(× 3 for the sealed scooter pack).

The ageing coefficients are the published values of Xu et al., not values
fitted to this cell.

The vehicle presets are typical published values for a Nexon-EV-class car and
an Indian electric scooter. They are parameters, so edit them in `ev_scenario.m`.

## Results to expect

### 1. Cell model against measured LG HG2 data

These tests were held out of the fitting: the first 2 h of the UDDS cycle at
each chamber temperature. The numbers come from the Python reference of the
same model. `run_Validation_LGHG2` reproduces them in Simulink.

| Chamber | Terminal-voltage RMSE | Surface-temperature RMSE | Measured self-heating | Predicted core − surface (max) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| −20 °C | 43.5 mV | 0.18 °C | 1.9 °C | 2.9 °C |
| −10 °C | 22.4 mV | 0.10 °C | 1.2 °C | 2.0 °C |
| 0 °C | 13.8 mV | 0.12 °C | 0.8 °C | 1.4 °C |
| 10 °C | 10.5 mV | 0.21 °C | 0.4 °C | 1.1 °C |
| 25 °C | 7.4 mV | 0.10 °C | 0.3 °C | 0.9 °C |
| 40 °C | 13.2 mV | 0.08 °C | 0.5 °C | 0.7 °C |

The fit errors on the identification data are 30, 18, 13, 9, 6 and 6 mV (from
−20 to +40 °C). The voltage error is largest at −20 °C, where the
charge-transfer nonlinearity is strongest.

### 2. Simulink equations against the Python model

The MATLAB Function and State-Space equations were emulated with the same
solver (ode4, 0.25 s). They match the Python model to within 0.02–1.2 mV RMS
on all six cases, and to within 0.006 °C on temperature.

### 3. Extreme heat (`run_Extreme_Heat_Scenarios`)

Compact e-car, 4 × MIDC (41 km), starting at 95 % SOC, air-conditioning on.
Expected values from the Python twin of the plant and BMS:

| Ambient | Wh/km | Max core temperature | BMS action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 40 °C (Jaipur summer) | 172 | 40.7 °C | none |
| 45 °C (Delhi heat wave) | 184 | 45.7 °C | **warning** for the whole drive |
| 50 °C (Rajasthan heat wave) | 205 | 50.7 °C | **warning + derating**: discharge limit cut to about 90 % |
| 55 °C (Thar desert) | 216 | 55.7 °C | **derating**: discharge limit cut to about 45 % (≈ 9 A per cell); charging stopped |
| 60 °C | – | 60 °C | **cut-off**: the battery may not supply the car |
| Scooter, sealed pack, 6 % climb, 48 °C | 62 | 49.6 °C | warning, just below derating |

Wh/km rises with heat mainly because of the air-conditioning load. On the
gentle MIDC the cell current (≈ 2.3 A) stays below the derated limit, so the
car still drives at 50–55 °C, but with much less power reserve for overtaking
or climbing. Fast charging at 50 °C ambient is refused by the BMS; at 45 °C it
runs with the warning on.

### 4. Indian climate scenarios (`run_Indian_Climate_Scenarios`)

Compact e-car, 6 × MIDC (62 km), starting at 95 % SOC. These are the expected
values from the Python twin. The Simulink run should agree closely.

| Scenario | Wh/km | SOC after 62 km | Max core temperature | BMS action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leh, −10 °C (heater on) | 214 | 51 % | −8.5 °C | none (charging would be blocked) |
| Bengaluru, 25 °C | 137 | 68 % | 25.6 °C | none |
| Delhi, 45 °C (AC on) | 184 | 59 % | 45.7 °C | over-temperature warning |
| Pack at 60 °C | – | – | 60 °C | **discharge cut off**: the vehicle is not allowed to drive |
| Western Ghats, 6 % climb (31 km) | 425 | 53 % | 37.2 °C | none |
| E-scooter, 45 °C, sealed pack | 28 | 74 % | 45.6 °C | warning |

The MIDC is a gentle cycle, so per-cell currents stay below 1C and
self-heating is small. The ambient temperature therefore dominates. Two
scenarios stress the electro-thermal part: the fast-charging cases and the 6 %
climb.
